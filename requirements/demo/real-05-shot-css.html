Krásná Lípa (Czech pronunciation: [ˈkraːsnaː ˈliːpa];
German: Schönlinde) is a town in Děčín District in
the Ústí nad Labem Region of the Czech Republic.
It has about 3,300 inhabitants. The town is located
on the border of three hilly natural regions and is a
gate to the Bohemian Switzerland National Park.
